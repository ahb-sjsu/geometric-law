## Running Example: Judge Rivera's Docket

Judge Elena Rivera sits on a federal district court. Every case on her docket requires navigating the judicial reasoning space — weighing precedent, applying constitutional constraints, balancing the rights of parties. Her decisions are geodesics on the legal manifold, shaped by the metric of precedent, constrained by constitutional boundaries, and guided by the heuristic of legal doctrine.
