## Key Supreme Court Cases

- *Brown v. Board of Education*, 347 U.S. 483 (1954). Racial segregation in public schools violates Equal Protection.
- *Carpenter v. United States*, 585 U.S. ___ (2018). Cell site location information is protected by the Fourth Amendment.
- *District of Columbia v. Heller*, 554 U.S. 570 (2008). Second Amendment protects individual right to bear arms.
- *Marbury v. Madison*, 5 U.S. (1 Cranch) 137 (1803). Judicial review of legislative acts.
- *Plessy v. Ferguson*, 163 U.S. 537 (1896). "Separate but equal" doctrine. Overruled by *Brown*.
- *Roe v. Wade*, 410 U.S. 113 (1973). Constitutional right to abortion. Overruled by *Dobbs*.
- *Students for Fair Admissions v. Harvard*, 600 U.S. 181 (2023). Race-based affirmative action in college admissions violates Equal Protection.
- *United States v. Booker*, 543 U.S. 220 (2005). Federal Sentencing Guidelines are advisory, not mandatory.
- *Wickard v. Filburn*, 317 U.S. 111 (1942). Commerce Clause extends to local activity with aggregate interstate effects.
